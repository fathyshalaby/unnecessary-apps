# Pigeon or Seagull?

Classify the bird from a chosen or newly captured photo—or three deeply scientific questions.

The app optionally analyzes a user-selected or newly captured photo with Apple Vision on-device, then falls back to a persisted manual checklist and ruling. Camera permission is requested only after the user taps Camera. The iOS Simulator may not provide Vision’s classifier runtime; the app labels that limitation and keeps the checklist usable. Photos are not uploaded or saved by the app, and the reset flow clears the local state.
