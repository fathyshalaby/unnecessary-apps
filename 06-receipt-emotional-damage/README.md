# Receipt Emotional Damage

Enter a purchase and receive the exact cost-per-intended-use invoice nobody asked for.

## What actually works

- Enter an optional purchase name, positive EUR amount, category, intended-use
  count, and your own planned/impulse label.
- The app publishes and applies one transparent formula: purchase amount ÷
  intended uses, rounded to cents. It does not apply arbitrary spending or
  affordability thresholds.
- Decimal commas, decimal points, and common European grouping are accepted;
  invalid or zero amounts keep filing disabled and show live validation.
- Up to 50 purchase reflections persist locally with purchase count, logged
  amount, and user-marked impulse summaries.
- A filed purchase can carry one optional 1–30 day local review reminder. The
  app asks for notification permission only when that reminder is actually
  filed, and deleting the purchase cancels its pending notification.
- Reminder delivery uses the shared local notification coordinator and adjusts
  into the collection's 09:00–20:30 quiet-hours window.
- Browse recent or complete history, delete one purchase, reset only the current
  receipt, or confirm complete local-data erasure.
- The result is rendered as an animated receipt-paper invoice with monospaced
  type, barcode and perforation details, rather than a generic result card.
- This is a manual reflection ledger, not budgeting, financial advice, or a bank
  connection.

No backend, account, analytics, ads, bank connection, or network permission.
Notifications are optional, local, one-shot, and tied to a purchase the user
explicitly asks to revisit.
