# Launch-week capture manifest

## Shared setup

- Phone set to Do Not Disturb with all personal notifications hidden.
- Clean neutral wall or desk; one strong brand-colored prop per episode.
- Camera vertical at 4K/30 or 1080p/30; lock exposure and white balance.
- Record voice close to the microphone; capture ten seconds of room tone.
- Screen recordings use fictional data and start with a clean status bar.
- Creator wardrobe remains consistent across the trailer and Episodes 1–3.
