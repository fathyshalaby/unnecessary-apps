# Microwave Sommelier

Package-time wattage conversion delivered with the confidence of a person who owns a tiny wine cellar.

## What actually works

- Enter the package time, wattage printed in the package instructions, and the
  wattage printed on your microwave.
- The app publishes and applies the proportional formula: adjusted time equals
  package time × package wattage ÷ microwave wattage, rounded to five seconds.
- Results include the exact source/target conversion and an 80% first
  checkpoint for checking or stirring before short additional bursts.
- Optional food names, current inputs, and up to 20 conversions persist locally.
- Browse recent or complete history, delete one conversion, reset only the
  current pairing, or confirm complete local-data erasure.
- The result converts timing only. It cannot measure temperature, doneness, or
  food safety and never overrides package or appliance guidance.

No appliance integration, backend, account, analytics, ads, or network permission.
