PRIVACY

Small apps. Small data footprint.

Effective date: 26 August 2026

The current Unnecessary Apps iPhone apps are local-first. We do not operate a service that receives your app content. There is no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, remote AI service, or public publishing system in the current releases.

What stays on your device

Some apps let you enter text, numbers, ratings, names, or journal entries. Those values are processed on-device and, where the app offers persistence, stored in that app’s local storage. The app’s Reset or Clear control removes the applicable local record.

Do Not Text Them keeps draft content in memory and stores only anonymous local counters. Apology Draft Generator keeps drafts session-only; its Copy action is user-initiated and hands text to the system clipboard.

Optional device features

Only the app you are using may request the feature it needs. You can deny a request and use the app’s manual fallback where one is offered.

  • Photos: Pigeon or Seagull? and Dog Name Guesser process a photo you select with Apple Vision on the device. Tiny Personal Museum stores its selected exhibit photo in that app’s protected local container until you reset it. We do not receive or upload these photos.
  • Microphone: Neighbor Noise Translator can sample the sound level for two seconds. Audio is processed in memory; no recording is saved or uploaded.
  • Location and maps: Public Bathroom Quality Map and Quiet Café Index use Apple MapKit to search the visible map for possible facilities or cafés, and Local Bench Reviews displays Apple MapKit content. When you tap one of these apps’ location buttons, optional When In Use access recenters the map near you. Saved coordinates, ratings, observations, and notes stay in the respective app’s local storage. We do not receive them or operate a public venue database. Apple’s processing of MapKit and location services is governed by Apple’s policies and your device settings.
  • Apple Health: Step Debt can read today’s step count, Sleep Alibi can read recent sleep samples, and Workout Excuse Detector can read today’s workout durations. Access is optional and read-only. Health data is used on-device for the requested result, is not written back, and is not uploaded or used for advertising.
  • On-device models: Bad Advice from a Peasant and Apology Draft Generator can use Apple Foundation Models when available. Processing remains on the device, with a deterministic local fallback.

Features we do not use

The current apps do not use contacts, calendars, WeatherKit, advertising identifiers, background location, or remote AI services. Health-themed outputs are entertainment or simple summaries, not diagnoses, treatment, coaching, or medical advice.

Information we collect

We do not collect app content or operate an account, analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting service for these releases. Apple may process App Store, backup, or device-diagnostic information under Apple’s own policies and your device settings; that information is not sent to a service operated by Unnecessary Apps Corp.

Children and sensitive information

The apps do not require an account and are not designed to collect information from children. Avoid entering confidential or identifying information. Never post medical reports, private messages, or other sensitive content in a public support request.

Changes

We will update this page before a released app begins collecting data or adds a materially different permission, remote service, or third-party SDK.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion help, use the support page. Because current app data stays on your device, you can normally remove it with the app’s Reset or Clear control or by deleting the app.
