# Social Battery Receipt

An itemized private log for the social energy you actually report.

## What actually works

- Record an optional event name, people count, duration, and your own 0–10
  energy scores before and after the event.
- The receipt reports the exact signed change, observed drain/recharge/break-even
  result, duration, and final self-reported status. It does not multiply people
  into an invented psychological score.
- Up to 30 receipts persist locally with event count, total logged time, and
  average energy change summaries.
- Browse recent or complete history, delete one receipt, void only the current
  report, or confirm complete local-data erasure.
- The app records subjective entries; it does not diagnose, predict, or decide
  how much socializing is healthy.

No account, backend, analytics, ads, or network permission.
