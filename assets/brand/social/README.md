# Unnecessary Apps social avatar

Use `unnecessary-apps-social-avatar-master.png` as the source file. The 1080,
400, and 128 px exports are ready for social profiles, previews, and small UI.

The mark intentionally removes the long corporate wordmark at avatar size. It
keeps the recognizable bored pigeon, green bureaucratic badge, and red chair
stamp. Keep the full `unnecessary-apps-corp-logo.png` for wide lockups and press
materials.

## Files

- `unnecessary-apps-social-avatar-master.png` — 1254 × 1254 source
- `unnecessary-apps-social-avatar-1080.png` — TikTok/Instagram/YouTube source
- `unnecessary-apps-social-avatar-400.png` — web profile and creator-kit export
- `unnecessary-apps-social-avatar-128.png` — small-avatar legibility check

## Generation prompt

Created from the existing pigeon mascot as an identity reference. The prompt
requested a square, wordless, circular social badge that stays legible at 40 px:
a simplified bored office pigeon in a white collar and green tie, holding one
oversized red rubber stamp with a chair pictogram; thick near-black outlines;
forest green, tomato red, cobalt, warm cream, charcoal, and pigeon grey; flat,
motion-ready mascot art with no tiny details or copied entertainment branding.
