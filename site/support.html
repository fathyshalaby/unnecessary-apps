SUPPORT

Something unnecessary misbehaving?

These apps are intentionally tiny and local. If a result looks absurd, that is usually the feature. If a control does not respond, relaunch the app and try its Reset or Clear control.

Include this in a report

  • App name and version/build
  • iPhone model and iOS version
  • Exact steps that produced the problem
  • Whether it survives relaunch
  • A screenshot with private information removed

Do not include passwords, private messages, medical reports, contact lists, faces, or other sensitive material. Reports are public, so redact screenshots carefully.

Permission problems

Photo, microphone, location, and Apple Health access is optional and only appears in apps that use it. You can review access in iPhone Settings. The HealthKit apps, Neighbor Noise Translator, Public Bathroom Quality Map, Quiet Café Index, and Local Bench Reviews retain manual fallbacks if access is denied. Successful Apple Health reads require compatible data to exist on the device.

Your data

Current releases have no account or cloud data store. Use the app’s Reset or Clear control to remove its applicable local record. Deleting an app removes its local app container from the iPhone. See the privacy policy for details.

Contact

Open a support request on GitHub. Use only a redacted screenshot and non-sensitive reproduction steps. You do not need a GitHub account to read existing notices, but GitHub may require an account to create a request.
