Use this as the starting point for the App Store Connect record. Keep the joke legible in search results; the app should feel like a tiny public service, not a generic utility.

- Subtitle: `Municipal seating intelligence`
- Promotional text: `Observe real chairs. File the evidence. Receive one needlessly official seating verdict.`

## Description

Finding a chair should not require a committee. Add chairs you encounter, rate
comfort, shade, and pigeon risk, then receive one decisive municipal verdict.
Build a shortlist, compare the contenders, and delete any evidence that no
longer serves the investigation.

Features:

- Create a shortlist of up to 20 observed chairs.
- Compare comfort, shade, and pigeon situation.
- Delete individual observations or erase the complete archive.
- Get one overconfident seating verdict based on your evidence.
- Enjoy a deliberately unnecessary level of municipal seriousness.

Just chairs, evidence, and an unnecessary amount of municipal seriousness.
