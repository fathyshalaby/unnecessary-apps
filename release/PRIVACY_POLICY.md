# Unnecessary Apps Corp — privacy policy

Effective date: 26 August 2026

This policy covers the current iOS apps in the Unnecessary Apps Corp
collection. Each app is deliberately small, local-first, and private by
default.

## Information the current apps handle

Some apps let you enter text, numbers, ratings, names, or journal entries.
Those values are processed on the device and, where the app offers persistence,
stored in that app’s local storage. The current apps do not send those values
to a server operated by Unnecessary Apps Corp. There is no account system,
advertising SDK, analytics SDK, remote AI service, or public publishing system
in the current releases.

Do Not Text Them intentionally keeps draft content in memory and only stores
anonymous local counters. Its optional completion notification is scheduled on
the device with generic text and never includes the draft. Apology Draft Generator keeps drafts session-only;
its copy action is user-initiated and hands text to the system clipboard. The
other apps’ local records can be cleared with their in-app reset or clear
control.

## Optional device features

Only the app being used requests the feature it needs. A user can deny a
request and use a manual fallback where one is offered.

- Pigeon or Seagull? and Dog Name Guesser process a user-selected photo with
  Apple Vision on the device. Tiny Personal Museum stores its selected exhibit
  photo in that app's protected local container until reset. These photos are
  not received or uploaded by Unnecessary Apps Corp.
- Neighbor Noise Translator can sample the microphone level for two seconds.
  Audio is processed in memory; no recording is saved or uploaded.
- Public Bathroom Quality Map and Quiet Café Index use Apple MapKit to search
  the visible map for possible facilities or cafés, and Local Bench Reviews
  displays Apple MapKit content. When a user taps one of these apps' location
  buttons, optional When In Use access recenters the map. Saved coordinates,
  ratings, observations, visit context, and notes remain in the respective
  app's local storage. They are not received by Unnecessary Apps Corp or
  published to a venue database. Apple's processing of MapKit and location
  services is governed by Apple's policies and the user's device settings.
- Step Debt can read today's step count, Sleep Alibi can read recent sleep
  samples, and Workout Excuse Detector can read today's workout durations.
  Apple Health access is optional and read-only. Health data is processed on
  the device for the requested result, is not written back, and is not
  uploaded or used for advertising.
- Bad Advice from a Peasant and Apology Draft Generator can use Apple
  Foundation Models when available. Processing stays on the device and each
  app has a deterministic local fallback.

The current apps do not use contacts, calendars, WeatherKit, advertising
identifiers, background location, or remote AI services. Health-themed outputs are
entertainment or simple summaries, not diagnoses, treatment, coaching, or
medical advice.

## Information we collect

We do not operate a service that receives the local app content. The current
binary has no account, login, tracking, advertising, crash-reporting, or
analytics collection. Apple’s own operating-system backup or diagnostic
features are controlled by the device and Apple’s policies, not by a server
run by this collection. We do not collect app content through those
Apple-operated features.

## Children and sensitive information

Do not enter confidential, identifying, or medical information unless you have
decided that keeping it on your own device is appropriate. Never post medical
reports, private messages, or other sensitive content in a public support
request. The apps are not designed to collect information from children, and
they do not require an account.

## Changes and contact

We will update this policy before a released app begins collecting data or
adds a materially different permission, remote service, or third-party SDK.
For privacy questions or deletion help, use the public support page:

https://fathyshalaby.github.io/unnecessary-apps/support.html

Because current app data stays on the device, it can normally be removed with
the app's Reset or Clear control or by deleting the app.
