# Hydration Narc

Enter your water intake and receive one passive-aggressive report from the bottle.

## What actually works

- A one-tap control records manual water servings, with an immediate one-step undo.
- The user chooses a personal 1–16 serving target; a serving can mean whatever
  amount they use consistently. The app does not prescribe an amount.
- The bottle’s report is generated from the current count and survives relaunch.
- The current ledger resets automatically on a new calendar day and the prior
  day enters a bounded seven-day local summary.
- “Empty today’s ledger” requires confirmation, clears only today, and preserves
  earlier daily summaries.

Apple Health water can be imported as a separate, read-only milliliter total.
It is intentionally not merged into the serving ledger because a serving is a
user-defined amount and Health data can be incomplete. An optional
quiet-hours-aware daily reminder is available. After an explicit connection, the
read-only total refreshes on relaunch; the manual serving ledger remains the
primary action.

All logging is on-device with no backend, analytics, ads, or network permission.
It is a personal ledger, not medical advice or a hydration recommendation.
