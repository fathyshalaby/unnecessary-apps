# Am I Early?

Tell the app when you arrived. It determines whether you are punctual, early, or causing a scene.

## What actually works

- A persisted signed arrival offset from 30 minutes late to 60 minutes early
  produces one of five transparent deterministic verdict levels.
- An optional occasion label turns each ruling into a useful private arrival record.
- Up to 30 arrivals persist locally with filed/not-late/late summaries,
  recent/full-history browsing, and individual deletion.
- Reset clears the current report without silently deleting history; confirmed
  complete erasure removes current inputs, verdict, and every arrival record.

No backend, account, analytics, ads, or network permission.
