# What Was I Doing?

Tap the button every time you open your phone and immediately forget why.

## What actually works

- Each tap records a timestamped lapse, a context category, and an optional
  120-character last-known mission.
- The latest four lapses show enough context to spot interruption patterns,
  and the summary names the most common category.
- Events are stored on-device and restored after relaunch.
- Individual deletion, erase-today, and confirmed erase-all controls are
  available from the app.
- The log is capped at 100 entries to keep local storage bounded.
- Existing timestamp-only records migrate as “Unspecified” without being lost.

This app has no account, backend, analytics, ads, or network permission.
