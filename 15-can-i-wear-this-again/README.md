# Can I Wear This Again?

A private closet ruling based on your own rewear rules and honest condition checks.

## What actually works

- Set completed wears since washing and your own maximum-wear limit.
- Mark odor, a visible stain, or a sweaty/intense previous wear; any marked
  condition flag sends the item to laundry.
- Days since last wear are reported only as a playful social-repeat check. The
  app explicitly says that resting time does not clean clothing.
- Optional item names, current evidence, and up to 30 filed rulings persist
  locally across relaunches.
- Browse the newest five or complete history, delete one ruling, reset only the
  current evidence, or confirm complete local-data erasure.

This is intentionally a manual personal-rule aid, not hygiene or medical
advice. It cannot see, smell, or inspect clothing and does not use photos,
laundry sensors, a backend, an account, analytics, ads, or network permission.
