# Overthinking Evidence Board

Build a tiny courtroom for your worry: evidence for, evidence against, and a less dramatic conclusion.

## What actually works

- Bounded prompts capture a worry, supporting evidence, counter-evidence, a
  less dramatic explanation, and one small next step.
- The app produces a transparent non-diagnostic status instead of pretending
  to know whether a worry is true.
- Current draft text survives relaunch and can be cleared without deleting the
  private archive.
- Up to 50 completed case files stay on-device, with recent/full-history views,
  individual deletion, and confirmed erase-all.
- The app is an everyday reflection prompt, not therapy, diagnosis, crisis
  support, a truth assessment, or a personal-safety decision tool.

No backend, account, analytics, ads, or network permission.
